The field, traced to its sources

Learning engineering is scattered across conferences, standards bodies, and grey literature. This is where it's linked together — topic by topic, claim by claim.

Browse by topic Open the topic map Search the corpus

Why this exists

01

Practitioners define the field differently depending on where they found it.

A conference talk. A standards committee. A blog post. The vocabulary doesn't converge. A claim made in one venue rarely gets traced back to its evidence, and the evidence rarely links forward to where it's actually used.

02

So every topic here links to what backs it up.

Seed papers. Expansion literature pulled from the wider citation graph. And the non-paper resources — programs, people, conferences, tools, organizations — that make the claim operational, not just cited.

03

Curated, then filtered, then verified.

Corpus filters, cross-seed scoring, and human review keep the noise out. What's here is meant to hold up when someone checks it.

04

This is a reference, not a pitch.

No claim without a source. No resource without a link back to where it came from. When ICICLE and this site differ on a definition, ICICLE wins.
