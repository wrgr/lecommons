Topic Map

How learning engineering connects

Eighteen topics and thirty-five concepts arranged by prerequisite flow. Foundation topics at the top feed into Practice, which feeds into Context. Click any node for details — including prerequisite chains, key papers, and linked resources.

Academic lens: follow the evidence, concepts, and source material behind each topic.

Practitioner lens: use the Practice topics to move from a local learning problem to a practical next step, with approaches that can be adapted for resource-constrained and developing-country contexts.
